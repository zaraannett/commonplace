Privacy Policy

Commonplace — last updated September 2026

Commonplace is a personal, single-user notes and planning app. It is not a public product — it has exactly one user account, belonging to the app's owner, and no one else can sign up or access it.

What data this app stores

Entries you create in the app (notes, tasks, diary entries, mood check-ins, habits, and similar content) are stored in a private database (Supabase), protected by row-level security tied to the owner's account. No other person or account can read or write this data.

Pinterest integration

If connected, the app reads pins from one Pinterest board the owner has explicitly authorized, using Pinterest's official API. Pin images and titles are cached in the same private database described above, purely to display them on the owner's own bulletin-board view inside the app. This data:

  • is never shared with, sold to, or visible to any third party
  • is only ever displayed back to the owner, inside their own private app
  • can be disconnected at any time by revoking the app's access from Pinterest's own account settings, at which point the app stops fetching new pins

What this app does not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics services
  • No selling or sharing of data with any third party
  • No public sign-up — access is limited to the owner's own account

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app owner directly.
